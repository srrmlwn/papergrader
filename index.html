Reading your document

Grading usually takes 20 to 60 seconds. Keep this page open.

Grades are written by Claude and can be wrong.

What happens to your writing

Your file is opened in your browser. Only its text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be graded, and the graded pages are drawn in your browser.

This app doesn't save your writing, your comments or your grades. Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models by default.

Don't upload anything you aren't allowed to share, like other people's private information.
